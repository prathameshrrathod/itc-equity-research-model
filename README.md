# ITC Ltd. - Equity Research & Financial Valuation Model (May 2026)

## **Executive Summary**
This repository contains a comprehensive, institutional-grade valuation model for **ITC Limited (NSE: ITC)**. The analysis focuses on deconstructing the company's conglomerate structure, specifically the high-margin "Tobacco Anchor" versus the scaling FMCG segment.

* **Intrinsic Value:** ₹254.73
* **Current Market Price (CMP):** ₹311.00
* **Recommendation:** **CAUTIOUS / HOLD** (-18% Downside)

## **Key Valuation Insights**

### **1. The "Tobacco Anchor" Dependency**
Despite significant revenue diversification, the Cigarette segment continues to contribute **~78% of consolidated EBIT** with superior margins of **~62%**. The valuation remains structurally anchored to tobacco-related regulatory and ESG risks.

### **2. FMCG Scaling vs. Margin Compression**
FMCG is the primary growth driver (11% CAGR); however, currently low margins (~8%) compared to industry peers like HUL (~22%) lead to consolidated margin compression as the segment increases its revenue share.

### **3. Intrinsic Truth (DCF Analysis)**
The DCF model utilizes a **11.29% WACC** and a conservative **4% Terminal Growth Rate**. The resulting value of ₹254.73 suggests the market is currently pricing in overly optimistic long-term FMCG margin expansion.

### **4. Relative Valuation Reality Check**
ITC trades at **~20.1x P/E**, aligning closely with tobacco peers (~24x) rather than FMCG giants (~72x). This confirms that a structural "conglomerate discount" persists.

## **Project Methodology**
* **Earnings Normalization:** Adjusted FY25 PAT to remove a **₹15,147 Cr** one-time gain from the hotel demerger.
* **Segmental Forecasting:** Built a 5-year revenue and EBIT forecast based on individual segment performance.
* **Equity Bridge:** Applied a **50% haircut** to non-current investments and accounted for minority interests to isolate shareholder value.
* **Sensitivity Analysis:** Modeled multiple scenarios (Bear/Base/Bull) to test valuation sensitivity to WACC and growth rates.
